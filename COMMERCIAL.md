## When you need a commercial license

- Your legal/policy team does not permit AGPLv3 software in your environment.

  the copyleft obligations of AGPLv3 — including offering it as a hosted or
  managed service (AGPLv3 §13 requires source disclosure to network users).
- You want contractual warranty / indemnification, which the AGPLv3
  explicitly does not provide.

## When you do NOT need one

- Running Oxidant inside your company to query your own data, unmodified or
  modified, as long as you comply with AGPLv3 (notably §13 if you expose a
  modified version to users over a network).
- Benchmarking, evaluating, contributing back.
